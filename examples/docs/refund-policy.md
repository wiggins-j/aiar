# Acme Widgets — Refund Policy

## Refund window

Customers may request a full refund within **30 days** of delivery. After 30
days, store credit may be offered at the discretion of support, but cash
refunds are not available.

## Eligibility

To be eligible for a refund the item must be unused and in its original
packaging. Digital goods (license keys, downloads) are non-refundable once the
key has been revealed.

## How to request

Email support@example.com with your order number. Refunds are processed to the
original payment method within 5–7 business days of approval.

## Shipping costs

Original shipping charges are non-refundable. Return shipping is paid by the
customer unless the item arrived damaged or defective.
